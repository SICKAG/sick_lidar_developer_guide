    Bulk Scan¶

    Compatible with: LRS4000 picoScan100

    LiDAR can measure the profile of woodchips or other bulk raw materials on conveyor belts. Mounted above the belt, the sensor continuously scans the material surface. The application determines material height relative to the empty belt.

    Example woodchip conveyor installation. The highlighted scan area covers the material profile across the belt.
