# SICK LiDAR Models in NVIDIA Isaac Sim

> Simulate SICK LRS4000, multiScan100, picoScan100, and MRS1000 LiDAR models in NVIDIA Isaac Sim before hardware is available.

Source: https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/simulation/nvidia-isaac-sim/

# NVIDIA Isaac Sim

Compatible with: LRS4000 multiScan100 picoScan100

<div class="tool-intro" markdown>

![NVIDIA](https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/assets/simulation/nvidia-logo.png)
NVIDIA Isaac Sim provides simulation environments for robotics, industrial automation, and autonomous systems. SICK LiDAR models let you test applications and algorithms before physical hardware is available.

</div>

SICK models are available for picoScan150, picoScan120, multiScan136, multiScan165, LRS4581R, and MRS1000. Find them on the applicable SICK product page under **Downloads > Software** or in NVIDIA Omniverse's sensor library. Configure the mounting position, orientation, field of view, scan frequency, detection range, and environment to match the deployment.
