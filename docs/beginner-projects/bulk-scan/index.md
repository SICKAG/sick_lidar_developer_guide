# Bulk scan with LiDAR

> Measure woodchip profiles on conveyor belts and combine cross-sectional area with belt speed to calculate bulk-material volume throughput.

Source: https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/beginner-projects/bulk-scan/

# Bulk Scan

Compatible with: LRS4000 picoScan100

LiDAR can measure the profile of woodchips or other bulk raw materials on
conveyor belts. Mounted above the belt, the sensor continuously scans the
material surface. The application determines material height relative to
the empty belt.

![LiDAR mounted above a conveyor belt carrying woodchips, with the scan area highlighted in purple](https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/assets/shared-features/woodchip-volume-conveyor.png)
