# Contact

Compatible with: LRS4000 multiScan100 multiScan200 picoScan100

Choose the contact option that best fits your question.

## Local Sales

Contact your local SICK sales representative for product selection,
application advice, pricing, and availability. Visit [sick.com](https://www.sick.com/)
and select your country or region to find the local sales contact.

## Support Portal Case

For technical troubleshooting, open a support case at
[support.sick.com](https://support.sick.com/). Describe the issue, your setup,
and the steps needed to reproduce it. Include the part number and serial
number from the device's type label, together with the firmware version.

If your device provides a diagnostic-file export, create the file through
its configuration interface and attach it to the case. Diagnostic information
helps SICK investigate the issue; the available export options depend on the
device and firmware.

## Public Discussion

For general integration questions and sharing experience with other users, join the
[GitHub Discussions space](https://github.com/SICKAG/sick_lidar_developer_guide/discussions)
or [start a new discussion](https://github.com/SICKAG/sick_lidar_developer_guide/discussions/new/choose).

Discussions are public. Do not post credentials, confidential project data,
or diagnostic files containing sensitive information; use a Support Portal
case for information that should not be shared publicly.
