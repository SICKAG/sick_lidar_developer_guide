# picoScan120 Starter Kit

> Three SICK products for a picoScan120 starter kit, with product photos, direct product links, and a link to getting started.

Source: https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/picoScan100/picoScan120/starter-kit/

This compact starter kit brings together a picoScan120 and the cables
needed to connect it to a development computer.
