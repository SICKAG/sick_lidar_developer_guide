# LRS4000 Starter Kit

> Three SICK products for an LRS4000 starter kit, with product photos, direct product links, and a link to getting started.

Source: https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/LRS4000/starter-kit/

This compact starter kit brings together an LRS4000 and the cables needed
to connect it to a development computer.

Together, these products are a good combination to get started with the
LRS4000. A suitable DC power supply is also required; see
[Electrical Connection](https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/LRS4000/electrical-connection/) for the supply requirements
and pin assignments. Continue with [Getting Started](https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/getting-started/)
to connect the sensor and open its web interface.
