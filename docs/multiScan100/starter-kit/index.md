# multiScan100 Starter Kit

> Three SICK products for a multiScan166 starter kit, with product photos, direct product links, and a link to getting started.

Source: https://sickag.github.io/sick_lidar_developer_guide/multiScan100/starter-kit/

to connect it to a development computer.
